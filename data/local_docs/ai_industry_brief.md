# Internal brief: investments and partnerships in generative AI

Microsoft has invested more than 13 billion US dollars in OpenAI since 2019, and OpenAI's models
are served through Microsoft Azure. OpenAI was co-founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk,
Ilya Sutskever, Greg Brockman and others; Sam Altman is its chief executive officer and OpenAI
develops ChatGPT and the GPT family of models.

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees, including siblings Dario Amodei and
Daniela Amodei. Dario Amodei is the CEO of Anthropic, which develops the Claude family of models.
Amazon invested 8 billion US dollars in Anthropic, and Google also invested in the company.

Nvidia, led by its co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang, supplies most of the GPUs used to train these
models and competes with AMD and Intel in the data-center accelerator market.

Mistral AI, headquartered in Paris, develops open-weight language models; Microsoft announced a
partnership with Mistral AI in 2024. xAI was founded by Elon Musk in 2023 and develops the Grok
chatbot; in 2025 xAI merged with X Corp., the company that owns Twitter.
